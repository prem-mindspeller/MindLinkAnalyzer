A dog named Max got lost during a thunderstorm. He ran through the rain until he did not know where he was. Max found a small barn and hid inside until the storm stopped. In the morning, he smelled fresh bread. It was coming from a bakery two streets away. Every Saturday, his owner brought bread home from that same bakery. So Max followed the smell all the way home. His owner was so happy to see him. She gave him a warm bath and a big bowl of food. After that day, Max always wore a collar with his name and address on it. He never got lost again.
