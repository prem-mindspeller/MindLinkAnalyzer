A boy named Sam planted tomato seeds every spring. One year, rabbits kept eating the young plants. Sam first tried covering them with cloth, but the wind blew it away. Next he built a small fence, but the rabbits dug under it. Then his grandmother gave him an idea. She told him to plant marigold flowers around the tomatoes, because rabbits do not like their smell. Sam planted a full circle of orange marigolds. This time, the rabbits finally stayed away. By summer, Sam had more tomatoes than ever before. He gave a basket of tomatoes to his grandmother to say thank you.
