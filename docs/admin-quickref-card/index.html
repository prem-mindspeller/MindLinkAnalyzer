② CONNECTING THE AMPLIFIER TO LAPTOP

  1. Press and hold the button on the BrainLink device for 2 seconds
  2. Wait for the LED to blink blue-red-blue pattern
  3. On your laptop, open Bluetooth settings and click "Add device"
  4. Select the device labeled "Brainlink_Pro (Audio)"
  5. The device will connect briefly and then disconnect — this is normal
  6. Open the MindLink app and log in — the actual connection is established through the app
  7. When successfully connected, the LED will change from blue-red-blue to solid blue
TIP
Connection Tip: The Bluetooth pairing is just the first step. The device will appear to disconnect after initial pairing, but this is expected behavior. The real data connection happens when you log into the MindLink app.

HEADBAND FITTING & DEVICE SETUP

③ HEADBAND FITTING

  • - Clean electrode contacts with a soft cloth
  • - Push hair aside from the forehead electrode area
  • - Moisten the electrode pad lightly (alcohol wipes)
  • - Place electrode ~2 cm above the center of eyebrows
  • - Strap snug but NOT tight — no red marks on skin
NOTE
Switch to Macrotellect soft-shell flex-band if:
  • If head is small and the fit is loose
  • Standard band keeps slipping after 2 re-seats
  • Signal stays RED or AMBER after 3 repositioning attempts
  • Student reports discomfort with the rigid band

SIGNAL QUALITY & NETWORK

④ SIGNAL QUALITY — TRAFFIC LIGHT

GRAY — Waiting

Normal on startup. Wait 5–10 seconds.

GREEN — Good

Proceed with calibration.

AMBER — Poor

Re-seat headband. Ask student to relax forehead & jaw.

RED — Not worn

Electrode not touching skin — reposition immediately.

FIX
If stuck on RED or AMBER:
  1. Moisten the electrode pad
  2. Re-seat lower on forehead (~2 cm above brow line)
  3. Clear hair from under the electrode
  4. Clean the skin with an alcohol wipe
  5. Switch to the soft shell flex-band

⑤ NETWORK / LOGIN ISSUES

  • - Windows security block: Right-click the .exe -> Properties -> check "Unblock" -> OK
  • - Firewall: Allow BrainCompanion.exe through Windows Firewall (both Private and Public)
  • - Required URLs (must be reachable via HTTPS):
    en.mindspeller.com   nl.mindspeller.com
  • - Campus / corporate Wi-Fi: Ask venue IT to whitelist the URLs above before the event

STUDENT FAQ & WELLBEING

⑥ COMMON STUDENT QUESTIONS

"Can I blink?"

YES — blink normally when performing eyes open calibration or tasks. Our software filters blink artifacts. Trying NOT to blink creates more noise.

"Can I move?"

Stay as still as comfortable. Avoid head turns, jaw clenching, or scratching during recording. Stretch between segments.

"What am I supposed to do?"

Follow the on-screen prompts. I'll tell you when to close or open your eyes. Just relax and pay attention.

"Is this safe?"

Completely. It only READS brain waves, sends nothing into your head. Just a simple sensor on your forehead.

"How long does it take?"

About 7–10 minutes. 30 s eyes closed, 30 s eyes open, then a few short tasks on screen.

"What if I mess up?"

No right or wrong — we measure your brain's natural response, not performance. Just do your best.

⑦ IF A STUDENT WANTS TO STOP

  1. Say calmly: "No problem at all."
  2. Click ✕ on the task window to end recording
  3. Gently remove the headband
  4. Reassure: "This is completely voluntary. Your data will not be used."
  5. Log the incident on the booth sheet (student initials + time)
NOTE
If anxious but willing to continue:
  • "It only reads, like a fitness tracker for your brain."
  • "You can stop at any time, just say the word."
  • "Just relax and breathe normally."
  • Offer water. Take a 30-second break before restarting.

NEVER force continuation. Student wellbeing is more important than data.

TROUBLESHOOTING — EMG / NOISE

⑧ EMG / NOISE ERRORS

If "High noise detected" appears on screen:

  • - Ask: "Can you relax your forehead? Try to unclench your jaw."
  • - Move student's phone / smartwatch >30 cm away from headband
  • - Check that laptop charger cable isn't draped near the headband
  • - Re-seat headband lower on forehead
  • - Wait 10 seconds, transient bursts often self-resolve
TIME
If noise persists >30 seconds:
  1. Power-cycle device (OFF → wait 5 sec → ON)
  2. Close the app completely and reopen
  3. Try the Macrotellect soft shell flex-band

WRAP-UP & DEVICE RECONNECTION

⑨ END OF SESSION

  1. Press the analyze button and mention that it will take a few minutes to complete
  2. When the Seed report button is active, click and confirm the student's email ID
  3. Wait for seeding to complete. Proceed after you see a success message
  4. Gently remove the headband
  5. Power OFF the device
  6. Wipe electrode & headband with an alcohol wipe
  7. Ask the student to go back to the browser, refresh the page, and press the Unlock button — neuroprofile generation will begin (takes ~4 minutes)
  8. Direct the student to the printer station — they will receive a business card with their neuroprofile and a unique QR code

Target turnaround between students: ~30 seconds

⑩ DEVICE RECONNECTION (MID-SESSION)

If the device disconnects during a session:

  1. Restart the application
  2. Switch OFF the BrainLink amplifier (hold power button 2 seconds)
  3. Wait 3–5 seconds
  4. Switch ON the amplifier and check the LED if it blinks blue-red-blue
  5. Log in again - this clears stale data and re-establishes the serial connection
LINK
If it still won't connect:
  • Turn off the device and wait 10 seconds
  • Restart the MindLink application entirely
  • Verify battery level is above 20%
  • Check if the LED blinks blue-red-blue, which means its ready to connect.
